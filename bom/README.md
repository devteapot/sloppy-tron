# Bill Of Materials

V0 target:

- Raspberry Pi 5
- two servos for pan and tilt
- servo controller or microcontroller bridge
- dedicated servo power rail
- wide-angle camera
- USB microphone or small mic array
- speaker or small amplifier board
- visible listening/mute indicator
- physical power switch or motor cutoff

Open selection questions:

- exact servo model
- camera module versus USB camera
- microphone array versus simple USB mic
- speaker and amplifier choice
- controller board or microcontroller bridge
- power rail capacity and protection
